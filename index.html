Who We Are

Kazi Robotics LLC is a technology company incorporated in Delaware, USA, with operations in West Africa. We believe Africa deserves world-class technology products built specifically for African needs.

Our name "Kazi" comes from Swahili, meaning "work" or "job" - reflecting our mission to create technology that does real work for real people.

We start with music because we believe everyone deserves access to great music, even without reliable internet. Our AI-powered offline platform brings African music to African ears.

Our Mission

To build world-class technology products that solve real problems for African users. We start where the need is greatest - connectivity, content, and communication - and expand from there.
